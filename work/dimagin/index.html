Overview

Dimagin is a creative collective that turns complex problems into clear digital products — I gave it a brand and a site to match.

I designed the identity and the multi-page site around a “soft tech / glass” direction: layered glassmorphism, a living aurora background and crisp display type. Home, work and contact, each with its own entrance.

The aim was a site that feels premium and modern the second it loads, while staying easy to read and to navigate.

(02)

The challenge

Most agency sites blur into one another.

To win trust, a studio's own site has to prove the craft it sells. The challenge was a distinctive visual language — glass, light, motion — that signals quality without getting in the way of the message.

So the system leans on depth and translucency, with restraint: one type system, a tight palette, and motion that guides rather than decorates.

(03)

Designed moments

01
The glass system
Layered translucent cards floating over a slow, living aurora gradient — depth and lightness without heaviness.
02
Page transitions
Each section rises in with staggered elements, so moving through the site feels fluid and app-like rather than like loading pages.
03
Living thumbnails
Every project preview is a small animated SVG scene, hinting at the product inside before you even open the case.
(05)

Outcome

A studio site that feels like a product itself.

The soft-tech glass direction gives Dimagin an identity that's instantly recognisable and unmistakably crafted — the kind of first impression that earns the discovery call.
